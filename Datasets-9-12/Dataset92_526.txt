Bailers in the V.I. 
baseball fun days 
The jaycobie Blash Foundation in 
conjunction with the V.I. Tourism 
Department will present the Bailers 
in the V.I. baseball fun days. The 
events will feature fun and baseball 
instrustion form minor and major 
league baseball players. Day 1 is 
Thursday at 4 p.m. at Lionel Roberts 
Stadium on St. Thomas. when chil-
dren ages 13 and up are the focus. At 
p.m. Friday, events move to Emile 
Griffith Ballpark on St. Thomas, 
where the focus will be on children 
ages 9 to 12. On Saturday, St. Croix 
Little Leaguers will be featured at 10 
a.m. at D.C. Canegata Ballpark. Food 
and drinks will be on sale. 
St. Croix Metric Mile 
ST. CROIX — The St. Croix 
Metric Mile Road Race will be held 
at 5 p.m. Saturday. Registration 
will begin at 4 p.m. at Anally Farms 
in Upper Love. The distance for the 
race is 1,500 meters and will start 
at St. John's Episcopal Church and 
finish at the Agriculture Fair 
Grounds. Awards will be presented 
to the top five male and female fin-
ishers and top boys and girls 13 and 
under. 
For information, contact the V.I. 
Pace Runners at 643-2557 or logon 
to: http://virginislandspace.org. 
Tackle football camp regis-
tration 
ST. THOMAS — Registration for 
the V.I. Youth Tackle Football Camp 
is at 10 a.m. on Saturday and Dec. 
at the CARS field. The camp is 
open to all youths in grades 5 to 9 
interested in learning how to play 
tackle football. For more informa-
tion or to register, call Francisco 
Janis at 244-8591. 
Froggy Johnson Basketball 
Tournament 
ST. THOMAS — Rosters are 
available for the seventh annual 
Ralph "Froggy" Johnson 13-16 
Double Elimination Basketball 
Tournament scheduled for Dec. 20-
at Alvin McBean Complex. The 
first eight teams to register will be 
eligible. Entrance fee is $10 per 
player, and entry deadline is 
Saturday. To pick up a roster or for 
more information. call Albelto 
Roberts at 777-3373. 
Women's soccer league 
ST. THOMAS —The U.S. Virgin 
Islands Soccer Association's St. 
Thomas Women's Soccer League 
takes place at the UVI soccer field 
every Sunday from 4 to 6 p.m. Any 
women interested in learning to play 
soccer are invited. 
For details, call the USVI Soccer 
Association office at 719-9707 or 
email usvisoccerftmail.com. 
Thompson wins STAR Jingle Bell Run 
By KEMP CALLWOOD 
Daily News Staff 
Michael Thompson completed the 
3-mile course at Magens Bay in 18 
minutes and 33 seconds to win the 
St. Thomas Association of 
Roadrunners' annual Jingle Bell 
Run on Sunday. 
Brad Kappel was second overall in 
20:23, followed by Jack Hullings in 
21:17, Michael Sheesley in 23:25 
and Steven LaPlace in 23:34. Ali 
Slimming (25:45), Tegan Kappel 
(26:58), Adrienne Baird (27:04) and 
Christine Sheehan (27:47) round out 
the top 10. 
St. Croix's annual Cane Bay 5-
mile Jingle Bell Run will be held at 
a.m. on Dec. 21. The run will 
start and finish at Cane Bay Beach 
on Northshore Road, and it will run 
along the shore to Renaissance 
Carambola Resort and back. 
Awards include a special Jan 
Mitchell armament / medal to the 
top 20 finishers, 10 turkeys — two 
turkeys to the top male and female 
finishers, two turkeys to the best 
costumed male and female and raf-
fle of the remaining six turkeys to 
the participants.

62. For information. logon to:
